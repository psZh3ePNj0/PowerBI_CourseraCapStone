<h2>Objective Overview: Dashboards, Alerts and Subscriptions</h2>

<br>

Impact of analytics is meaningful by $$\color{blue}curating \space data \space and \space setting \space up \space proactive \space measures$$. Goals here are: 
- Create an $$\color{blue}executive \space dashboard$$ in Power BI to curate data efficiently. 
- $$\color{blue}Display \space sales \space summaries$$ focusing on product quantities and median sales trends.
- $$\color{blue}Highlight \space profit \space metrics$$, emphasizing net revenue and Yearly Profit Margin by country.
- Use card visualizations for $$\color{blue}quick \space insights$$.
- Ensure the dashboard is $$\color{blue}mobile-friendly$$, making important data accessible.
- $$\color{blue}Configure \space subscriptions$$ to empower stakeholders with timely and actionable insights.
- Set up $$\color{blue}proactive \space alerts$$, enabling them to steer their strategies precisely and confidently.
